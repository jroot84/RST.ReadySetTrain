Privacy Policy

Short version: RST has no server, no account system, and no analytics. Everything you enter — your routine, progress, and profile — stays in your browser, on your device.

What this app is

RST • Ready Set Train is a static web app. It is a single page that runs entirely in your browser. There is no backend server, no database, and no user accounts — Claude and the developer of this app have no way to see, collect, or receive anything you enter.

What's stored, and where

RST saves the following using your browser's local storage (localStorage), under the key rst:v1:

  • Your workout routine and exercise library
  • Calendar completion history
  • Profile details you choose to enter (height, weight, gender, fitness goal) if you use the optional "Check My Workout" feature
  • Your theme preference (dark or light)

This data never leaves your device automatically. It is not transmitted to any server, and the app has no way to access it from another device unless you move it yourself.

Backup & export

The Settings tab lets you export your data as a JSON file and import it later. That file is created and saved locally by your browser — it only goes wherever you choose to save or share it.

What RST does not do

  • No analytics or tracking scripts
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No accounts, sign-in, or passwords
  • No collection of your name, email, or location
  • No cookies used for tracking

Third-party services

RST loads a small number of resources from outside services in order to function:

  • Google Fonts — page fonts are loaded from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google may log standard request data such as your IP address when fetching these files, per Google's own privacy policy.
  • YouTube — exercise entries include an optional link that opens a YouTube search in a new tab for that exercise's proper form. RST does not embed video or share any data with YouTube; once you click through, YouTube's privacy policy applies to that visit.
  • GitHub Pages — this app is hosted as a static site on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log standard web server access data (such as IP address and browser type) for anyone visiting the site, per GitHub's own privacy statement.

Deleting your data

Since everything lives in your browser, you're always in control:

  • Use the reset options under Settings to clear progress, your routine, or your exercise library individually
  • Clear your browser's site data/local storage for this page to remove everything at once
  • Uninstalling the app (if added to your home screen) or clearing browser data has the same effect — nothing persists elsewhere, because nothing was ever sent elsewhere

Children's privacy

RST is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, regardless of age — it doesn't collect information from anyone at all, since it has no server to collect it with.

Changes to this policy

If this app's functionality changes in a way that affects this policy (for example, adding a server-side feature), this page will be updated and the effective date above will change accordingly.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the GitHub repository this app is published from.
